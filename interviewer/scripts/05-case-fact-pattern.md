### Fact pattern: advise the client.
- type: case
- reading: 10m
- budget: 10m
- fact:
  A regulated entity has been filing quarterly reports late for three consecutive
  quarters. Each filing was eventually made, and no investor complaint has been
  received. Internal counsel says the delays are caused by a system migration that
  finishes next month. The regulator's enforcement staff have asked for a meeting.
  You act for the entity. What do you advise, and in what order?
- key:
  Strong answers lead with the recommendation before the reasoning; separate the
  immediate step (respond to the meeting request, preserve records) from the
  remediation story (migration timeline, controls); name the risk of characterising
  a repeated breach as a technical issue; and say explicitly what they would need to
  know before advising further.
