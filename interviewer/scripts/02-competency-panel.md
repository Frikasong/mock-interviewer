# Competency panel / 结构化能力面
<!-- Structured, scored, public-sector style · 7 questions | 政府/监管机构常见的打分式 · 7 题 -->
### Tell me about a time you had to interpret an unfamiliar rule or policy under time pressure.
- type: behavioural
- budget: 150s
- watch: name the source you went to first

### Describe a situation where you disagreed with a supervisor or senior colleague.
- type: behavioural
- budget: 150s
- watch: no more than two sentences of background

### Give an example of managing competing deadlines.
- type: behavioural
- budget: 150s
- watch: say what you deprioritised, not just what you finished

### Tell me about a mistake you made and what happened next.
- type: behavioural
- budget: 150s

### Describe a time you had to explain something technical to a non-specialist audience.
- type: behavioural
- budget: 150s

### You discover an error in work that has already gone out. What do you do?
- type: situational
- budget: 150s
- framework: criteria first, then the call, then the fallback

### What do you understand this organisation's mandate to be, and where do you see it under pressure?
- type: employer-specific
- budget: 150s
